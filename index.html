Approach

Prepared people. Clear roles. Useful partnerships.

Effective disaster work begins before an operation is activated. Volunteers need clear expectations, realistic training, safe practices, dependable communication, and an understanding of how their capabilities fit into a larger incident structure.

Austin's personal approach is to support practical readiness, reduce avoidable uncertainty, and build relationships before communities face their worst days.
